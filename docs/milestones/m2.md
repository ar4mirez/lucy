# m2: full replacement, part 1 (every org)

## Goal

Every org runs on lucy. 48-78 PRs, about 1.2-4.3B.

## Scope (in build order)

1. Multi-org supervisors, per-org credentials, cross-org refusal (LUCY-ORG-001).
2. Seat pools, quota-aware placement, failover and return, budgets (LUCY-ORG-002).
3. Offboard and erase; key rotation and encrypted backups (LUCY-CRED-006, LUCY-SEC-011).
4. Resource-aware scheduler (LUCY-ORG-003).
5. Knowledge model (LUCY-ORG-004).
6. Client-confirm posture, demotion triggers, handoffs, Adapter conformance (LUCY-ORG-006,
   LUCY-SPEC-018).
7. `lucy import` from the old supervisor and moving the four client orgs, each after its corpus is green and 20
   consecutive tasks land (LUCY-ORG-005, LUCY-ORG-007, LUCY-CORP-004).
8. Detection rules and the incident workflow (LUCY-SEC-009, LUCY-SEC-010).

## Out of scope

The owner surface and retiring the old supervisor (m3).

## Exit tests

1. All five orgs run on lucy with the corpus green for each (LUCY-CORP-004, LUCY-ORG-007).
2. A cross-org read attempt is refused and audited (LUCY-ORG-001).
3. A forced usage limit fails a task over to another seat and back (LUCY-ORG-002).
4. Offboarding an org provably erases its data (LUCY-CRED-006).
5. Detection rules fire on synthetic incidents; an incident drill freezes and restores an org
   (LUCY-SEC-009, LUCY-SEC-010).
6. Adapter conformance passes (LUCY-SPEC-018).

## Security

4 checklist items land in m2: 4.3, 5.2, 5.3 and 5.4, plus the key-rotation follow-up of 3.7
([checklist map](../security/checklist.md)).

## Open questions

  reach the Pilot unattended (LUCY-RUN-010). m1 keeps corpus rule channel-05 as written: a
  supervisor only proposes, and only the owner's words close a decision, under every posture.
  Delegating that is an owner decision for m2.
